# SocialBot board v4 — art direction

Taxonomy: print broadsheet / racing program. Long-document ledger, one entry per weekday, ~780px measure. Cream paper, ink type, rust used as a thread, not a fill.

1. Ledger, not board. Five entries stacked under a 2px ink rule, hairline between days. The page reads top to bottom like a program, and the eye never has to choose between columns.
2. Copy is the layout. Day numeral → subject → one-line italic blurb → full LinkedIn draft in Source Serif at 18px. The draft sits in document flow at natural height. No box, no max-height, no inner scrollbar, ever.
3. Two registers, one thread. Bricolage Grotesque for subjects, Source Serif for anything read, IBM Plex Mono caps at 10px for anything filed. Big Shoulders italic numerals in rust carry the day and the queue rank. Rust appears nowhere else except the active tab rule and the edit link.
4. X is a tab, not a box. Mono radio tabs under a hairline. Flip shows the full X text at the same size and measure, with a character count and an over/under mark in the foot line.
5. Edit in place. Edit turns the draft into a borderless textarea with a rust left rail, auto-grown to content. Save writes localStorage, stamps "Revised HH:MM" and offers "Restore generated". Almanac counts update.
6. Metadata under a fold. A dashed hairline and a "+ Filed" mono summary. Open it and you get a label/value grid. Closed by default because the words that ship come first.
7. Decisions are typographic. Approve / Hold / Kill are mono ghost links with an underline for the chosen one. The status plate at the top of the entry changes to match. No filled buttons, no colored chips.
8. Queue under the week, ranked. Story queue is the same ledger idiom: rank numeral, serif title, mono kind tag, Promote as a ghost link. It follows the week rather than competing beside it.

Kill list from v2/v3: five-across day grid, nested DRAFT scrollers, 999px pills, gradient wash on the paper, soft drop shadows, rounded cards, Palatino body, filled Approve/Kill buttons, a separate "Your decisions" panel, the "build:" version pill.

Avoid: purple or blue accent anywhere, white surfaces, glass or blur, emoji, icon sets, centered hero, sans-serif body copy, sidebars or split panes, modals for editing, a top nav.
